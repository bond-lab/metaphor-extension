# 2026 sheet (template)

Generated by `scripts/build_animal_sheet.py` from the 2024 and 2025 exports;
not yet filled in by students. Import `animal-metaphors-2026.xlsx` into Google
Sheets (File > Import > Replace spreadsheet) to create this year's sheet, or
import the CSVs one tab at a time.

```bash
uv run python scripts/build_animal_sheet.py --year 2026 \
    --language "Japanese=data/animal-metaphors/2026/prefill/ja.csv=Japanese Wordnet 2.1" \
    --language "Czech=data/animal-metaphors/2026/prefill/cs.csv=Czech Wordnet 3.0 + ARASAAC" \
    --language "Slovak=data/animal-metaphors/2026/prefill/sk.csv=Slovak Wordnet 2.0" \
    --language "Korean=data/animal-metaphors/2026/prefill/ko.csv=ARASAAC"
```

The `prefill/` CSVs come from `scripts/prefill_animal_metaphors.py` run on
`../2025/English.csv` (`--wordnet wnja:2.1`; `--wordnet cswn:3.0 --arasaac cs`;
`--wordnet omw-sk:2.0`; `--arasaac ko`).

## Tabs

- `English`: the 95 omw-en pairs of the 2025 master list, each followed by the
  metaphors students added for that animal in 2024 or 2025, then the animals
  that are new to the list (alphabetical). Columns beyond the usual ones:
  - `Source`: `omw-en` for the original pairs; otherwise the tab the metaphor
    came from, the word the students wrote, their Y/N in brackets and their
    comment after `--`.
  - `Previous`: Y and N counts over the nine tabs that made real judgements.
  - `Animal sense`: for added metaphors, the master list's sense for that
    animal, else the most frequent animal sense of the word in omw-en. Blank
    for phrases such as "old fox" or "cat and dog".
- `Japanese`, `Czech`, `Slovak`, `Korean`: the same rows with `Language`
  pre-filled from the latest translation earlier students of that language
  gave, else from the wordnet/ARASAAC pre-fill (then `Comment` starts with
  `auto:`). `Previous` lists that language's earlier Y/N per year, with
  `(default)` for the untouched 2024 tabs. `Checked (Y/N)` is blank everywhere.
- `Languages`: investigator names (empty) and data sources.

Once students have filled the sheet in, re-export it with
`scripts/fetch_animal_sheets.py` (add the new sheet id) and these files will be
replaced by the real data.
